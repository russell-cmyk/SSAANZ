Join the SSAANZ Community

Becoming a member of SSAANZ connects you to a nationwide community of veterans, serving personnel, and their whānau who understand the unique challenges and experiences of service life.

Membership helps you stay informed, stay connected, and access the support you need — whether you’re currently serving, transitioning, or long since discharged.

As a member, you’re part of a growing network committed to Unity Through Adventure, community connection, and veteran wellbeing across Aotearoa.

SSAANZ membership also strengthens our ability to deliver navigation support, host community events, and expand regional engagement so more veterans and whānau can access the help they deserve.

Why Membership Matters

Membership with SSAANZ is more than a sign‑up — it’s a commitment to staying connected, supported, and part of a community that understands the realities of service life.

For Veterans

Membership helps maintain connection long after leaving the NZDF, ensuring you’re never navigating challenges alone.

For Serving Personnel

It provides a bridge between military life and the wider veteran community, offering support during transitions and throughout your career.

For Whānau

It creates a network of understanding, shared experience, and community strength.

Your membership also strengthens SSAANZ’s ability to deliver navigation support, host community events, and expand regional engagement across Aotearoa.

Every member helps us grow a national network grounded in our kaupapa of Unity Through Adventure, where shared experiences and community connection improve wellbeing for all who serve.

By joining SSAANZ, you’re not just becoming a member — you’re becoming part of a movement that honours service, supports whānau, and builds a stronger veteran community across Aotearoa.

Membership Options

Veteran Membership

$5/month or $60/year

For former service members who want to stay connected, access support, and be part of a national veteran community.

Serving Personnel

Free

For those currently serving in the NZDF who want to connect with SSAANZ, attend events, and access support during and after service.

Senior Veterans

$2/month or $40/year

A reduced‑rate membership for older veterans who want to remain part of the SSAANZ community.

Benefits

  • Access to Navigation & Support

    Get personalised guidance to help you understand and access services, entitlements, and wellbeing support for you and your whānau.

  • Invitations to Community Events

    Be the first to hear about SSAANZ events across Aotearoa — from regional gatherings to community activities grounded in our kaupapa of Unity Through Adventure.

  • Member Updates & Newsletters

    Stay informed with updates on events, support opportunities, regional activities, and community stories.

  • Connection With the Wider SSAANZ Community

    Join a supportive network of veterans, serving personnel, and whānau who share similar experiences and values.

  • Support the Mission

    Your membership helps SSAANZ grow its national reach and deliver more support to those who need it.
