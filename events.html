Community Events Across Aotearoa

SSAANZ hosts community events throughout the year to bring veterans, serving personnel, and their whānau together in meaningful, supportive, and enjoyable ways.

Our events are designed to strengthen connection, reduce isolation, and build a sense of belonging within the veteran community — no matter where you live in Aotearoa.

Every gathering reflects our founding kaupapa, Unity Through Adventure.

Whether the event is outdoors, social, or community‑based, we believe shared experiences help create lasting bonds, build confidence, and support wellbeing for those who have served and their families.

Our events are inclusive, family‑friendly, and welcoming to all ages and abilities. From regional meet‑ups to national gatherings, each event is an opportunity to reconnect, make new friendships, and be part of a supportive community grounded in service and whanaungatanga.

What Our Events Offer

Opportunities to Connect

Meet other veterans, serving personnel, and whānau in a relaxed, supportive environment.

Activities That Promote Wellbeing

Events are designed to encourage confidence, resilience, and positive mental and physical wellbeing.

Regional Gatherings

Local events strengthen community networks and ensure support is accessible across Aotearoa.

A Welcoming Environment

All events are inclusive and suitable for all ages, abilities, and stages of service or transition.
