Veterans, Serving Personnel and their Whānau Across Aotearoa

SSAANZ is a national, veteran ‑ led charity supporting current and former service members and their whānau across Aotearoa.

We provide navigation support, community events, and regional engagement to ensure every veteran and serving member can access the help, connection, and wellbeing support they deserve.

Our mission is to build a strong, connected, and supported veteran community, backed by supporters and partners who believe in the wellbeing of those who serve.

Our Core Services

  • Personalised navigation and support for veterans, serving personnel, and whānau
  • Community events across New Zealand that build connection and belonging
  • Regional engagement and local activation to strengthen veteran communities
  • A growing national network of supporters and partners committed to veteran wellbeing
