How We Manage Your Donation

SSAANZ is committed to managing every donation with transparency, integrity, and accountability. As a veteran‑led charity, we understand the importance of trust — and we honour that trust by ensuring your contribution is used where it makes the greatest impact for veterans, serving personnel, and their whānau.

Our Commitment to Transparency

  • Donations are allocated directly to programmes that support veteran wellbeing.
  • All spending is monitored, reviewed, and aligned with our strategic priorities.
  • We maintain clear financial reporting and responsible governance practices.
  • We prioritise cost‑effective delivery to ensure maximum impact for every dollar received.

How We Ensure Impact

  • Funds are directed to essential services such as navigation support, community events, and regional engagement.
  • We regularly assess programme outcomes to ensure they meet the needs of veterans and whānau.
  • We work with trusted partners and volunteers to extend our reach across Aotearoa.

Your donation helps us continue our mission of Unity Through Adventure, strengthening connection and support for those who have served.
