Our Supporters

SSAANZ is proud to stand alongside businesses, organisations, community groups, and individuals who support the wellbeing of veterans, serving personnel, and their whānau across Aotearoa.

Our supporters play a vital role in strengthening the veteran community. Their contributions help us deliver navigation support, community events, and regional engagement grounded in our kaupapa of Unity Through Adventure.

Together, we are building a stronger, more connected network for those who have served — and for the families who stand beside them. By partnering with SSAANZ, organisations demonstrate meaningful social impact, align with a credible national charity, and contribute directly to veteran wellbeing across the motu.

Supporter Impact

Every supporter helps create real, measurable impact for veterans and whānau across Aotearoa. Through your generosity, SSAANZ can:

  • Provide personalised navigation support for veterans and families
  • Deliver inclusive, community‑building events nationwide
  • Strengthen regional networks so support is accessible locally
  • Reduce isolation and improve wellbeing for those who have served
  • Expand our national footprint and reach more veterans in need

Your support directly contributes to a healthier, more connected veteran community — one where no one has to navigate life alone.

Why Partner With SSAANZ

Partnering with SSAANZ is an opportunity to make a meaningful difference. Organisations choose to support us because:

  • We are a credible, national charity

    Veteran‑led, community‑driven, and committed to delivering real impact across Aotearoa.

  • Your support creates measurable social impact

    Every contribution strengthens wellbeing, connection, and support for those who have served.

  • You align with a respected kaupapa

    Our founding principle, Unity Through Adventure, reflects connection, resilience, and community.

  • You gain visibility and recognition

    Supporter badges, logo placement, event recognition, and partnership opportunities help showcase your commitment.

Partnership Tiers

SSAANZ recognises and celebrates the generosity of our partners through three supporter tiers. Each tier reflects the level of contribution and the impact made on the veteran community.

Gold Supporters

Our Gold Supporters provide significant contributions that enable SSAANZ to expand national programmes, strengthen regional networks, and deliver high‑impact initiatives for veterans and whānau.

Gold partners receive premium visibility, event recognition, and priority partnership opportunities.

Silver Supporters

Silver Supporters help sustain our core services and community events across Aotearoa. Their support ensures veterans and whānau have access to connection, guidance, and community wherever they live.

Bronze Supporters

Bronze Supporters contribute to the ongoing delivery of SSAANZ’s mission and help us maintain a strong, connected veteran community. Their support is acknowledged through website listing and supporter recognition.
