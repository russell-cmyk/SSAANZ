Who we are

SSAANZ is a veteran‑led charity dedicated to supporting veterans, serving personnel, and their whānau across Aotearoa.

We were founded on a simple but powerful belief: that no one should navigate life during or after service alone.

Our kaupapa, Unity Through Adventure, reflects the origins of SSAANZ — bringing people together through shared experiences that build trust, connection, and belonging.

While our work has grown beyond adventure‑based activities, this principle continues to guide how we create community and support across the motu.

Today, we provide navigation support, community events, and regional engagement to ensure every veteran and whānau member can access the right help at the right time.

What We Do

SSAANZ provides practical, personalised support to help veterans and whānau access the right services at the right time. Our work focuses on three core areas:

Navigation & Support

  • We walk alongside veterans, serving personnel, and whānau to help them understand and access services, entitlements, wellbeing support, and community resources.
  • Our approach is confidential, compassionate, and tailored to each person’s needs.

Community Events

  • Connection is at the heart of wellbeing.
  • Our events bring people together — from casual meet‑ups to regional gatherings — creating opportunities to reconnect, build friendships, and strengthen community.
  • These events are grounded in our belief in Unity Through Adventure, where shared experiences help reduce isolation and build resilience.

Regional Engagement

  • We work across Aotearoa to activate local communities, strengthen regional networks, and ensure veterans and whānau have support close to home.
  • Our regional leads and volunteers help create a sense of belonging wherever veterans live.

Our Commitment

We honour service by supporting those who serve — with compassion, respect, and community at the heart of everything we do.
